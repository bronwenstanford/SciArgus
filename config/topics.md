#Aquatic Ecosystems and Climate Change
Assessments of likely impacts of climate change on rivers, streams, lakes, and wetlands. This includes assessments of climate change impacts to water quantity, timing of runoff, and water temperature, and their likely impact on species assemblages, species ranges, and ecosystem condition. Analysis of potential aquatic ecosystem climate refugia and how these can be identified are particularly welcome. 

#Groundwater-Dependent Ecosystems
Research to identify groundwater-dependent ecosystems, using remote sensing and field based methods.

#Environmental Flows
Examples of processes to develop environmental flows and frameworks and guidelines for how to set protective environmental flows. In addition, assessments of adopted environmental flows and the effect of environmental flows on ecological outcomes are particularly welcome. In addition, I am particularly interested in environmental flows incorporating functional flows.

#Water Supply and Climate Change
Thought leadership and analysis on how climate change will affect global or regional water supply, and analysis of effects on both human and non-human water availability.

#Salmon ecology 
Research on Pacific salmon and steelhead trout life history, response to climate change and pollution, and particularly response to changes in flow.

#Machine Learning to Model Streamflow
Papers describing attempts to model daily or monthly streamflow using machine learning techniques, including neural network models. Particularly interested in modeling efforts at large scales (regional, national).

#Managed aquifer recharge
Efforts to quantify environmental benefits and impacts of managed aquifer recharge, and suggestions for how to constrain recharge so that it can have environmental benefits.

#Conservation Science
Thought leadership and perspectives on the current state of conservation science, including evaluations of what is and is not working in global implementation and policy. This section welcomes reflections on the directions taken by conservation actors (governments, NGOs, and private entities), analyses of past successes and failures, and strategic visions for the future of the field.
